# Benchmark claim

A superiority claim requires the same frozen cases, hardware class, warm/cold policy, concurrency, timeout, provider versions, and cost accounting. Include abstentions in overall accuracy and publish coverage separately. Use paired bootstrap intervals where possible. Do not claim general superiority from synthetic smoke tests or a single domain; phrase results as applying to the measured workload only.
